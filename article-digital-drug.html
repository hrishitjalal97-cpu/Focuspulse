01 / THE LOOP

CHECK.
SCROLL.
REFRESH.

The loop is simple. Trigger curiosity. Deliver novelty. Remove the novelty. Trigger curiosity again.

The important part isn't the content. The important part is the possibility that the next piece of content might be interesting.

YOU ARE NOT BORED.
YOU ARE OVERSTIMULATED.
02 / THE COST

NOVELTY
DESTROYS DEPTH.

Deep work feels strange after constant stimulation. Reading ten pages feels slow. Sitting with a difficult problem feels uncomfortable. Silence starts feeling like something is missing.

This is where many people make the wrong conclusion. They think they have lost discipline. Sometimes they have simply trained their brain to expect interruption.

03 / THE RESET

MAKE BOREDOM
NORMAL AGAIN.

You don't need to disappear into a mountain. Start smaller.

Eat without your phone. Walk without headphones. Wait without opening an app. Work without checking messages every four minutes.

Give your attention enough silence to become yours again.
