FOCUSPULSE / 003

You keep blaming yourself for losing focus. Maybe that's the wrong diagnosis.

01 / DIAGNOSIS

DON'T FIX
WILLPOWER.

FIX THE
ENVIRONMENT.

Willpower is expensive. Every distraction you place directly in front of yourself becomes another decision you have to win.

Your phone is within reach. Notifications are enabled. Every application wants an update. Every platform wants another minute. Every interruption creates another context switch.

Then, when you fail to concentrate, you call yourself undisciplined. That's not a very good system.

YOUR ENVIRONMENT
IS PART OF YOUR
PRODUCTIVITY SYSTEM.
02 / THE PROTOCOL

MAKE
DISTRACTION
EXPENSIVE.

A better system removes the decision completely. If something consistently steals your attention, increase the friction between you and that thing.

Notice what happened. None of these actions required you to become a more disciplined person. They simply changed the default environment.

YOUR DEFAULT ENVIRONMENT
CREATES YOUR DEFAULT
BEHAVIOUR.
03 / FRICTION

ADD FRICTION
WHERE IT
MATTERS.

Friction is not always bad. In fact, friction can be one of the most powerful tools in your attention system.

PHONE MOVE IT.

Don't keep it on your desk. Distance turns an impulse into a decision.

WEBSITES LOG OUT.

Make the next visit require an intentional action.

VIDEO REMOVE THE SHORTCUT.

Don't let the easiest option automatically become the default.

WORK ONE TASK.

Make the desired behaviour require fewer steps.

Make the unwanted behaviour require three extra steps. Make the desired behaviour require one.

04 / DEFAULTS

DESIGN
THE DEFAULT.

The best productivity system is often the one you don't have to remember.

If your desk already contains everything required to start working, starting becomes easier. If your phone is already buried in another room, checking it becomes harder.

THE ENVIRONMENT
SHOULD PUSH YOU.

STARTING WORK 1 STEP
CHECKING PHONE 3+ STEPS
OPENING DISTRACTION HIGH FRICTION
FOCUS SESSION LOW FRICTION

The environment should quietly push you toward the person you are trying to become.
